Science and curiosity games for ages 5–8

Pick a question to explore together. You and your child can each make a guess, try something, and see what happens.

  • Angelfish Adventure

    Build an angelfish’s meal, find a hiding spot, and change the air in its swim bladder to see what happens.

  • Octopus Escape

    Play a free octopus camouflage game together. Change color and texture, try different hiding spots, and see what helps the octopus escape.

  • Shark Night

    Make predictions about sharks, sort facts from movie myths, and compare your size with a megalodon.

  • Feed the White Tiger

    Compare white and orange tigers, choose food, and explore how a tiger’s coat changes what a deer can see.

  • Giant Squid Dive

    Predict what a giant squid looks like, then change your submarine’s depth, light, and lure to look for it.

  • Zoo Kitchen

    Build a plate for a zoo animal, see what it eats, and swap an ingredient to try again.

  • Poohsticks Night

    Choose a stick and a river, predict what will happen, and change one thing before you race again.

  • Z Hunt

    Find E and Z in a little story, count what you spot, then look for letters around you.

  • Ocean Sound Explorer

    Listen to real ocean animal calls, guess who made each sound, and compare what you heard.

  • Make the Moon

    Move a simple Moon simulation, see how its appearance changes, and then look for the real Moon.
