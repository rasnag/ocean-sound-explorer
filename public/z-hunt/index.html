Step 4 · Why is Z so shy?

Three clues from the case file

  • The Romans kicked Z out.Long, long ago, the Romans took Z out of their alphabet. Later they let it back in, but it had to stand at the very end of the line. That's why Z is still last today.
  • Z is worth 10 points.In a word game called Scrabble, Z gets 10 points, one of the biggest scores. Rare letters are worth more because they're hard to use.
  • Hindi has a visiting Z too.In Hindi, ज़ is ज with a little dot under it. The dot was added for words that came from Persian, like ज़मीन (zameen, land).
