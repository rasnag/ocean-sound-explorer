Shark Night

A play-together page for Your child and Grown-up. Your child is the Pokémon expert. Grown-up is the shark detective. Each of you teaches the other one thing.

About 10–15 minutesHand the phone back and forth
Station 1 · Your child teaches

Your child's Pokémon class

Grown-up, you're the new student. Ask these and really listen. It's fine not to know anything.

  • What is a Pokémon? Is it an animal?Let him explain it his way.
  • Is there a shark one? Can you show me?There is one. Let him find it.
  • How do you know which one is stronger?Follow up: "Can a strong one ever lose?"
  • Which one would you be, and why?
Station 2 · Grown-up's surprise

Real or Movie?

Read each card out loud. Your child guesses first, then tap his guess to see the answer.

Station 3 · Walk it out

How long is a megalodon?

Guess first: how many of Your child's footsteps long is a megalodon? Then reveal and pace it out down the hallway or outside.
