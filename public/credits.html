For grown-ups

Listen to the animal by itself, then listen to a ten-second ocean mix and answer Yes or No. All five calls are real recordings. The mystery tracks are playful collages, not authentic recordings of these animals together in one place. Every expedition has three “yes” and two “no” rounds in a shuffled order.

Replaying a round keeps its mix and answer unchanged. Each new expedition varies the animals, entrances, overlapping calls, distances and volumes. Recordings are bundled with the game; internet is needed to load the page and audio. No account, microphone access, tracking or personal data collection.

There is no automatic spoken narration. Animal calls are real NOAA recordings. If sound is paused after switching apps or locking a phone, tap play again. Keep your device volume comfortable.
