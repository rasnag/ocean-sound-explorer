For grown-ups

This experiment uses four confidence-building animal rounds before one ocean mystery. Each round plays a real animal recording and asks the child to choose among three animals. Spoken directions are generated by the device's speech synthesis.

If sound is paused after switching apps or locking a phone, tap play again. Keep your device volume comfortable. No account, microphone access, tracking or personal data collection.
