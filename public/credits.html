Sounds & credits

All animal calls are real recordings. Owl, dog, and horse are used for two easy practice choices before the three ocean-animal rounds.

Recordings are bundled with the game. No account, microphone access, tracking, or personal data collection is used.

Practice recordings

  • Oriental scops owl: Otus_sunia.ogg, public domain; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3.
  • Horse neigh: Wiehern.ogg, public domain; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3.
  • Dog bark: Barking of a dog.ogg by Amada44, CC BY-SA 3.0; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3.

Ocean recordings

Source: NOAA Fisheries — Sounds in the Ocean: Mammals (2023).

  • Bottlenose dolphin: NOAA Fisheries, Northeast Fisheries Science Center, Passive Acoustics Branch.
  • Seal: Male elephant seal vocalization by Hullwarren, CC BY-SA 3.0; converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3.
  • Whale: humpback whale song from NOAA Fisheries, Northeast Fisheries Science Center, Passive Acoustics Branch.

Files are preserved locally so browser privacy and cross-origin restrictions cannot interrupt the game. Playback uses short excerpts with volume normalization; speed and pitch are unchanged.
