Camouflage activity · Ages 5–8 · About 10 minutes

Can you hide an octopus?

A paper octopus is easy to spot on the kitchen table. Can you make it harder to find without covering it up?

Start the activity Get the printable card
🐙
🐙

What you’ll need

  • Two matching paper octopuses and two backgrounds. Use the printable pieces below or make your own.
  • A third background for round two. Try fabric, newspaper, or a drawing.

Make a guess. Then try it.

  1. Make two predictions. Which background will make the octopus harder to spot? Each of you picks one and says why. It’s fine if you disagree.
  2. Take turns hiding. While one person looks away, the other puts one octopus in plain sight on each background. Look back from the same spot. Which one do you see first? Swap roles and try again.
  3. Change one thing. Keep the octopuses the same and swap one background for your third one. Before you look again, make a new guess. What changed your mind, if anything?

Camouflage can make an animal harder to notice when it blends in with what’s around it. Ask each other, “What gave our octopus away?” It might have been its outline, a difference in color, or the shadow under one of its arms.

What would you try next?

Would your best hiding place still work if the octopus moved? What if you looked from farther away? Pick one question and try it together.
