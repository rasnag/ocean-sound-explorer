# Ocean Sound Explorer — Decision Log

Use this file for product or technical decisions that should survive across chats.

## 2026-09-15 — Optimize the first experience for a five-year-old

Decision:
The game should begin with obvious, familiar animal sounds before introducing harder ocean sounds.

Why:
Early confidence is more important than maximizing difficulty. A child who fails immediately is less likely to continue.

Implications:
- Owl and Horse are warm-up rounds.
- Warm-ups come before ocean-animal rounds.
- Warm-ups use fewer answer choices.

## 2026-09-15 — Ask the child to identify the animal

Decision:
Use direct language such as “Which animal made that sound?” rather than framing clips as a mysterious recording.

Why:
The interaction should be immediately understandable without a parent having to explain the premise.

## 2026-09-15 — Give spoken guidance

Decision:
Core instructions and round transitions should be narrated in addition to being shown visually.

Why:
The primary user may not be a fluent reader yet.

## 2026-09-15 — Separate testing from exploration

Decision:
Optional sounds after mission completion should be presented as learning/exploration, not additional scored quiz rounds.

Why:
Finishing the mission should feel complete and successful while still providing a path for curiosity.
